CHENNAI — India’s Directorate of Enforcement has provisionally attached the land and five-storied building of Hotel Le Royale Palace in Karaikal, Puducherry, valued at approximately ₹12.64 crore ($1.49 million), after a cross-border referral from the French government traced the property to alleged gold smuggling, hawala, and illicit cryptocurrency transfers.

Announced Nov. 26 by the agency’s Chennai Zonal Office-II under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002, the attachment follows searches conducted on Oct. 28, 2026, at three premises across Karaikal, Puducherry, and Chennai, where investigators seized digital devices and documents.

From France to Puducherry via Dubai Agents

According to the Enforcement Directorate, the investigation was launched on the basis of credible information received from the Government of the French Republic concerning alleged cross-border offences committed abroad by Mougamadou Nizamoudine and his three sons — Mohamed Mubeen Nizamoudine, Shahine Shafique Mougamadou Nizamoudine, and Rashine Nizamoudine.

“Mougamadou Nizamoudine and his sons carried out smuggling of gold, hawala and illegal cryptocurrency transfers from France between 2019 and 2023 on a commission basis. ... These funds were then integrated into the financial system through the purchase of land and the construction of Hotel Le Royale Palace, Karaikal.” — Directorate of Enforcement, Chennai Zonal Office-II (Nov. 26, 2026)

N.R.E. Deposits and Brick-and-Mortar Integration

Investigators allege that from 2019 onward, commission proceeds generated in France were routed into an Indian Non-Resident External (NRE) bank account through foreign-currency cash deposits and wire transfers sent from the United Arab Emirates via intermediary agents, before being deployed to buy coastal land and construct the five-story hotel in Karaikal, a former French enclave in Puducherry.

Because the action is a provisional attachment order issued during an ongoing PMLA investigation, the allegations have not been adjudicated at trial and the individuals named retain the presumption of innocence.
